\## Summary of Major Gaps



The most frequently occurring gaps are:



1\. Quantitative potency data

2\. Quantitative selectivity data

3\. Assay-specific evidence

4\. Detailed human pharmacokinetic data

5\. Detailed safety/toxicity evidence

6\. Complete human ADME information



\## Interpretation



These gaps do not mean that the compounds lack scientific evidence.



They indicate that the current project dataset does not contain sufficiently structured or quantitative evidence in these areas.



The evidence-gap analysis is therefore a documentation and research-prioritization exercise, not a clinical ranking.



\## Priority



\### High Priority



\* Quantitative potency

\* Quantitative selectivity

\* Assay-specific evidence



\### Medium Priority



\* Human PK/ADME information

\* Detailed safety evidence



\### Candidate-specific Priority



Sutezolid requires particular attention because its evidence confidence is currently Moderate and its human ADME and mature safety evidence are incomplete.



\## Conclusion



The Phase 6 review shows that the dataset has strong basic target, mechanism, and development information for most candidates, while quantitative potency/selectivity and some PK/safety fields require additional evidence collection.
