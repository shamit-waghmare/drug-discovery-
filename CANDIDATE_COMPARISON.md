# TB Candidate Compound Comparison

## Purpose

This document compares 10 tuberculosis-related candidate compounds using a predefined evidence-based scoring framework.

The scores represent the strength and completeness of documented evidence available in the research dataset. They do not represent clinical superiority, therapeutic recommendation, or probability of successful drug development.

## Scoring Scale

Each criterion is scored from 0 to 5.

0 = No evidence
1 = Very limited evidence
2 = Limited evidence
3 = Moderate evidence
4 = Strong evidence
5 = Very strong evidence

## Criteria

1. Target relevance
2. Mechanism evidence
3. Potency evidence
4. Selectivity evidence
5. ADME evidence
6. Safety evidence
7. Research/development evidence
8. Evidence completeness

Maximum possible score: 40.

## Important Limitation

Scores are analytical assessments based on documented evidence in the dataset. They must not be interpreted as proof that a higher-scoring compound is clinically superior.
